Straight answers

FAQ

Is the free server real?

Yes. When you create one, we start an actual Paper server in its own container with 1 GB of heap and a real address. The console and controls talk to that server, not a demo.

Why does it sleep?

To keep the free plan free. An empty server past fifteen minutes is stopped and its memory returned to the machine. It boots again the moment you press Start.

Can my friends join?

Your server has its own address. Connect over a direct (non-proxied) host — the panel shows you exactly what to type.

How many servers can I run?

One free server per account, and Endhost runs a fixed number at once so no single server starves the others. If every live slot is busy, the panel tells you rather than overselling.

What about the paid plans?

They're launch pricing, shown honestly. There's no checkout yet, so nothing on this page will charge you. Follow the Discord to hear when they open.
